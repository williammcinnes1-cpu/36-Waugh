# Site photos — 36 Waugh Street, Griffith (taken from the rear of the lot, looking to the street)

The client's five site photos were shared in the chat but have not been saved as files yet. Put the
image files in this folder when they're uploaded. What they show:

- **View**: from the high rear of the lot there is a wide, open view south-east and east over Griffith
  town and the flat irrigation plains to the horizon (low hills visible to the east/north-east).
- **North-east neighbour** (left of frame when facing the street): single-storey white rendered house with
  a red/orange terracotta-tile hip roof, close to the shared boundary and sitting lower than this lot —
  the view passes over its roof. Shared fence: beige/cream Colorbond, about 1.8 m, on a low brick plinth
  at the rear. A red-roofed octagonal gazebo, a large gum and a tractor are on the land beyond the rear
  north-east corner.
- **South-west neighbour** (right of frame): sage-green Colorbond fence (about 1.8 m, stepped down the slope)
  with a large red-flowering bottlebrush and other trees behind it; a tile-roofed house beyond.
- **Across the street**: two-storey white rendered house with a dark grey hip roof and a garage/brick base;
  single-storey brick-and-tile houses next to it.
- **The lot**: vacant, long dry golden grass on red-brown soil, falling toward the street. A dirt vehicle
  track runs from the upper part of the lot down to the street (toward the south-west end of the frontage
  in the photos) — check whether an existing kerb crossover is there.

## Design implications
- Upper-floor living facing the street (SSE) and east captures the view; the slope means even the
  ground floor at the rear looks over the street.
- Upper windows facing the north-east neighbour need privacy treatment (high sills, fixed louvres or
  obscure glazing on the side facing their yard) while keeping the long view.
- All designs place the driveway at the low north-east end of the frontage; if the existing crossover is
  at the south-west end, a new crossover needs Council approval (or the driveway moves).
